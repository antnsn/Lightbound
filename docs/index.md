## Why Lightbound exists

A paladin keeps track of a lot at once. Blessings on every class, which paladin blesses what, your aura, Righteous Fury when you tank, seals and Judgements, Cleanse, Divine Intervention when a wipe comes. Missing one of them is easy, and in a group somebody notices.

Lightbound puts all of that in one addon. It buffs for you with a single key, sorts out the work with the other paladins, and reminds you about the things a paladin forgets. You don't need to know anything about addons to use it: install it, pick a key, and play.

## What it does

- **One buff key.** Out of combat, each press casts the next thing that's needed: Righteous Fury, your aura, then your blessing on the next group member who is missing it. [More](features/buff-key.html)
- **Blessings window.** One grid that decides who blesses what, shared with the other paladins in your group. [More](features/blessings-window.html)
- **Tank guard.** Never Blessing of Salvation on a tank. [More](features/tanks.html)
- **Judgements.** Who keeps which Judgement on the boss, and an icon in combat when yours is missing. [More](features/judgements.html)
- **Cleanse grid.** See who carries a debuff you can remove, and click to cleanse. [More](features/cleanse.html)
- **Cooldowns and Divine Intervention.** Every paladin's protective cooldowns, and a quick Divine Intervention picker for raids. [More](features/cooldowns-and-divine-intervention.html)
- **Warnings and trackers.** Missing aura, Righteous Fury, seal timer, Hammer of Wrath and Exorcism alerts, Reckoning stacks and more. [More](features/warnings-and-trackers.html)

## Why pick it over PallyPower?

PallyPower is the classic tool for sharing out blessings, and many paladins know it. Lightbound does that job too, and works alongside it, so nobody in your group has to switch at the same time.

What Lightbound gives you on top of blessing assignments:

- **It casts for you.** One key buffs the whole group, in the right order, and checks range. You don't click through class buttons.
- **It looks after tanks.** Salvation is kept off tanks automatically, and the group leader's tank list is shared with the other paladins.
- **It covers the rest of the paladin's job.** Judgement assignments, Cleanse, cooldowns, Divine Intervention, warnings and trackers, all in one place with one options window.
- **It's built for Forever.** It keeps working when Forever hides information in combat, and it knows Forever's spells and talents.

{: .note }
Lightbound speaks PallyPower's language. Paladins who still run PallyPower see you, can assign you, and get your assignments. If you have both installed, Lightbound copies your PallyPower assignments and stays quiet until you disable PallyPower. See [Getting started](getting-started.html#coming-from-pallypower).
