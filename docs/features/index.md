# Features

Lightbound does a lot of small paladin jobs for you. Each page below covers one of them: what it does, how to use it, the settings in the `/lb` window, and the things that surprise people. New to the addon? Start with the [buff key](buff-key.html) and the [Blessings window](blessings-window.html). Those two do most of the work.

- [Buff key](buff-key.html): one key that casts Righteous Fury, your aura and every blessing your group needs.
- [Blessings window](blessings-window.html): who blesses which class, shared with the other paladins (and with PallyPower).
- [Tanks](tanks.html): never Blessing of Salvation on a tank.
- [Judgements](judgements.html): who keeps which Judgement on the boss, with icons in combat.
- [Raid overview and requests](raid-overview-and-requests.html): who is missing your blessing, and players who ask for one in chat.
- [Cleanse](cleanse.html): a grid of your group that lights up when someone has a debuff you can remove.
- [Cooldowns and Divine Intervention](cooldowns-and-divine-intervention.html): every paladin's big cooldowns, a Divine Intervention picker and chat announcements.
- [Warnings and trackers](warnings-and-trackers.html): Righteous Fury, aura, seal, your own blessings, combat alerts, the trackers row, Well-Rested and Well Fed.
- [Quality of life](quality-of-life.html): reagent shopping, the minimap button, and moving and resizing frames.
